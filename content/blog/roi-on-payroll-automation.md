For many small to medium enterprises (SMEs) in India, the transition from manual Excel-based payroll to a fully automated HRMS feels like a daunting expense. However, when analyzed through the lens of cost-recovery, **payroll automation usually pays for itself within the first 6 months.**

## The Hidden Costs of Manual Payroll

### 1. Human Error & Compliance Penalties
The average error rate in manual payroll processing is 1.2%. If a company with 100 employees pays an average of ₹40,000/month, a 1.2% error rate equates to **₹48,000 lost monthly** due to overpayments or manual calculation bugs.

Furthermore, missing a PF or ESI deadline due to manual oversight triggers compounding interest penalties.

### 2. Resource Drain
A dedicated HR executive typically spends 5 to 7 days every month exclusively on payroll reconciliation and statutory report generation. That is **25% of their working hours** devoted to tasks a machine can do in seconds.

## Calculating Your Automation ROI

To calculate your true ROI, consider the following formula:

**ROI = (Net Savings - Cost of Automation) / Cost of Automation**

1.  **Calculate Net Savings**: Add the cost of standard human errors + the time value of your HR staff saved + CA consultation fees for filing PF/ESI.
2.  **Subtract Software Cost**: For example, yfy.ai costs ₹40/user/month.
3.  **The Result**: SMEs switching to yfy.ai typically see an ROI of **400% to 600%** in year one.
