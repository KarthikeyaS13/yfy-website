The Indian government has consolidated 29 central labor laws into 4 new **Labour Codes**. While the implementation date has seen delays, organizations must proactively audit and prepare their hr policies and payroll structures.

## The 4 Labour Codes at a Glance

### 1. Code on Wages, 2019
The Wage Code universally applies to all employees in organized and unorganized sectors. A critical change is the new definition of "Wages."

*   **Key Impact**: Basic salary must be at least 50% of the gross remuneration. 
*   **Action Required**: Overhaul compensation structures to ensure allowances do not exceed 50%. This will inherently increase Gratuity and PF liabilities.

### 2. Code on Social Security, 2020
This code extends social security benefits to gig workers, platform workers, and unorganized sectors.

*   **Key Impact**: Mandatory registration and contribution to gig worker welfare funds. 
*   **Action Required**: Companies relying on heavy freelance or gig models must restructure their engagement budgets.

### 3. Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020
Focuses on the welfare and safe working conditions of employees, particularly in factories, mines, and construction.

*   **Key Impact**: Introduces single registration for all establishments. Standardizes leave policies.
*   **Action Required**: Align current leave accumulation and encashment policies with the new standardized norms.

### 4. Industrial Relations Code, 2020
Aims to streamline the dispute resolution mechanism and provide flexibility in hiring and firing for certain sectors.
